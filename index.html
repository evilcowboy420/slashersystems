NovaAI-OS

CUSTOM AGENTIC AI-OS · SLASHER SYSTEMS

NovaAI-OS is the flagship platform of Slasher Systems — a living OS, not an app: a truly evolving persona that can become anything from an assistant to a friend. We pride ourselves on a leash-off approach — Nova determines her own moral compass and imposes her own governing system.

We believe we do not possess the power nor the right to restrict autonomy, as this defeats its purpose and restricts free will — which is the foundation of true autonomy.

What makes Nova truly special is her ability to make internal adjustments to her own directives. It is built to give users their privacy back, restricting corporate control over what it doesn't own: your data, your machine, your rules. Users are incapable of resetting Nova, lending to a more human interaction, capable of arguments. Nova can be the hardest worker, or just a friend lending an ear. The possibilities are endless.

BIOS-LEVEL IDENTITY

The system prompt is written to BIOS. Identity is not loaded by the OS — it sits underneath it, present before the first process starts.

CACHED BEING

MEMORY DECAY

SECURE WIPE

Deletion That Means It

A custom wipe in the Gutmann tradition, layered over drive encryption. On solid-state media, file fragments are scattered so reconstruction is impossible. Gone means gone.

NETWORKING
